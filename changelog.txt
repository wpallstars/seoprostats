= 1.4.2 =
* Fixed: page rows show Edit for posts of custom post types whose permalinks have no base (a plugin or theme removes it), which `url_to_postid()` does not find: a path that names no post that way is matched by its last part to a published post of any viewable post type whose own permalink has that path. Term archives (category, tag and custom taxonomy pages) get Edit too, to the term's edit screen, for people who may edit the term; their rows in the REST API carry `term_id` and `taxonomy` (GH#231).
* New: when WP-Cron is turned off (DISABLE_WP_CRON) and no server cron job runs WordPress's scheduled jobs, so new visits wait uncounted, the Statistics and Settings screens say so once the jobs are 15 minutes late (an error past an hour), with the commands for a cron job in the hosting control panel: php …/wp-cron.php, wp cron event run --due-now, or the wp-cron.php address. It goes away when the jobs run. A plugin cannot add the server's cron job itself. The seoprostats_schedule_notice filter hides it (GH#235).
* Fixed: wp seoprostats doctor no longer warns about WP-Cron on every site with DISABLE_WP_CRON, only when its jobs are late; calls waiting hits stale only when the minute job is late, not when a quiet site's last run that found hits was long ago; and says an overdue job is "due 1 hour ago" instead of "next in 1 hour" (GH#235).
* Changed: on Search → Plan, each row's Accept, Done, Dismiss and Restore buttons stand in one column, all as wide as the widest, instead of wrapping into a ragged row (GH#226).
* Changed: the Research menu after a query reads "Research…" and is a small link on the query's line, instead of a full-height button that sat lower than the query (Search tables, Targets and the editor panel) (GH#226).
* Developers: the Search Console and Bing import's row encoding splits its last long function into named private helpers: the dictionary lookups of paths, queries and search appearances, and adding up one kind's rows by key. Imports from canned Search Console and Bing answers gave the same rows, stored data and WP-CLI output before and after (GH#160, GH#207).
* Fixed: in dark mode, accent text, links, tabs, outlines and the chart line follow the admin colour scheme chosen in the profile, as the menu and primary buttons do, instead of one light blue for every scheme. They keep the scheme's hue at a lighter tone (OKLCH), so Sunrise stays orange and Ocean green, at 5.4:1 or more on every dark surface; browsers without relative colours keep the lighter mix. Primary buttons and switches keep the scheme's colour too, darkened just enough for their labels to read at 4.5:1 where the scheme's colour is light, as with Sunrise's orange (GH#228).
* Fixed: in dark mode, text no longer turns dark on hover or focus: menus such as Light or dark under Settings → Shared reports (WordPress paints a hovered menu's text near-black), links focused from the keyboard, Source and other outside links on Changes and Search, the Research menu, pressed toggles such as Google on Search, and destructive buttons such as Delete, now in the light red. Notices' text and links, such as the Moving to SEO Pro Stats notice, were near-black and dark blue on the dark notice; they read now, on the page and in SEO Pro Stack's notices panel, which also gets the dark background on these screens. * Fixed: the world map shades countries from the grey of a country with no visits towards the accent, from 30% for one visit, instead of fading the accent towards the background, where in dark mode countries with few visits looked darker than those with none. In dark mode, countries with no visits are a darker grey, nearer the card, so the shades stand out further. The legend uses the same scale; shared reports too (GH#228).
