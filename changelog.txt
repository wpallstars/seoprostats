== Changelog ==

Every change to SEO Pro Stats. readme.txt lists the latest version in short.

= Unreleased =
* Changed: Settings → Connections walks through the Search Console setup in six steps, each linking to the Google page it needs (new project, Search Console API, service accounts, keys, Search Console users), with what to choose on each and what to do if an organisation policy blocks keys. The README has the same steps.
* New: a Search section (Rankings), with its tab and menu item: Google Search Console's clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for the period against the previous one, a chart of the one you pick with the changes lane, and the queries, pages, countries and devices, sorted by clicks. Choose a page for its queries or a query for its pages. The period ends at the newest final day (about three days ago) and the comparison has as many days, so days not imported yet never look like a drop. Before Search Console is connected it links to Settings → Connections. REST route search, wp seoprostats search and, on WordPress 6.9 and later, the ability seoprostats/search; demo data has made-up search data too. Reads only the imported search tables, by their keys; never on visitors' pages.
* New: Pages in the Clicks report lists clicks, dead-click rates, links, forms and visits per page. Choose a page to narrow the section, choose again to clear it, and use View page or Edit (only with permission). REST clicks kind=pages and page_info; wp seoprostats clicks pages and page details.
* New: refunds from WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads 3, FluentCart and ThriveCart join the original purchase visit, once per refund. Purchase goal and property revenue subtract them per currency without increasing purchase completions; refunds never invent visits after retention.
* New: statistics collection without cookies: a collector that runs without loading WordPress (or the REST route on the WordPress.org build), daily-salted visitor hashes, and a processor that turns hits into visits, pageviews, events and properties every minute.
* New: reports for scripts and AI agents: the REST API (stats, timeseries, breakdown, realtime, markers) and wp seoprostats commands, with ranges, comparisons and filters.
* New: the SEO Pro Stats menu (under Dashboard) and its Overview: headline metrics with a chart against the previous period, Sources, Pages, Locations and Devices, filters by clicking any row, live visitors, and a view kept in the address so it can be bookmarked. Follows the admin colour scheme; every chart has a table for screen readers.
* Changed: the settings screen is SEO Pro Stats → Settings, the last item in the plugin's own menu, and no longer also under WordPress's Settings menu. Old links to it still work.
* Changed: when SEO Pro Stack organises the admin menu, the SEO Pro Stats menu stays at the top under Dashboard, with the other site statistics, instead of under Administrators.
* New: a Dashboard widget with today so far against yesterday at the same time and the last 7 days' visitors.
* New: the tracker, printed inline on front-end pages (about 2.5 KB compressed, no cookies or browser storage): pageviews with single-page app navigation, visible time and scroll depth, outbound links, file downloads, and events from seoprostats('Name', {...}) or data-sps-event attributes. Editors, feeds, previews and the customizer are not counted; filters add excluded paths and Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control.
* New: demo data: a Demo data switch on the Overview shows a little over a year of made-up visits, kept in tables of their own, apart from your live statistics; the Dashboard widget follows it. wp seoprostats demo, and data=demo on the reports.
* New: Goals, Funnels and Properties sections, with tabs and menu items: conversions, conversion rate and revenue per currency for each goal; visits and drop-off at each funnel step in order within one visit; property keys and values with revenue. Administrators add, change and delete goals and funnels; demo data has its own examples. REST routes goals, funnels and properties, and wp seoprostats goals, funnels and properties.
* New: choosing a row that already filters the reports removes the filter.
* New: click and form autocapture, and a Clicks section with its tab and menu item: clicked elements, dead clicks, link destinations, file links and forms sent, on the site or one page. Never what anyone types; emails and long numbers in labels are hidden, and data-sps-mask hides an element's text. Affiliate links (Settings → Tracking paths, or rel="sponsored") send an Affiliate link event. Clicks are kept 3 months (Settings → Data). REST route clicks and wp seoprostats clicks; demo data has clicks too.
* New: pages not found, site search, content and logged-in visits. The Pages card has a Not found tab (404s by address, to find broken links); a Site search card shows what people searched for and the searches that found nothing; a Content card shows views by author, category and post type; the Devices card has a Logged in tab. Search words are kept with emails and long numbers hidden, and Settings → Tracking can leave them out. All are filters and breakdowns in the REST API and WP-CLI (not_found, search, no_results, author, category, post_type, login), and demo data has them too. Visitor pages still run no queries: the page says which post it shows, and the minute job looks up its author and category.
* New: purchases. Paid orders from WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads and FluentCart become a Purchase event with the order's total in its currency, on the visit that placed it, once per order; ThriveCart orders too, through its webhook and the page's ID on ThriveCart links. No order numbers or customer details are kept. Settings → Tracking → Record purchases; seoprostats_purchase filter; wp seoprostats doctor lists the shops recorded.
* New: a change log of what changed on the site, when and on which page: posts and pages published, unpublished, moved, retitled and edited (words added and removed, internal links, external sites linked); SEO titles, descriptions, robots and canonicals from Yoast SEO, Rank Math, SEOPress and The SEO Framework; WooCommerce prices, sales, stock and coupons, and Easy Digital Downloads prices; plugins, themes and WordPress installed, updated, activated or switched; search engine visibility, permalinks, site address and front page settings. REST routes markers (now filled, for the charts) and changes, and wp seoprostats changes, with page and kind filters; seoprostats_record_change filter; demo data has changes too. Recorded when the change is saved, never on visitors' pages; the tables update by themselves.
* New: a Changes section, with its tab and menu item, and a markers lane under the Overview's chart: what changed in the period, coloured by group (content, SEO, products, site, notes), for the site or one page; markers are buttons that list their changes and open Changes. Administrators add and delete notes for what the log cannot see, such as a newsletter sent. REST routes POST /annotations and DELETE /annotations/{id}, wp seoprostats annotate, and on WordPress 6.9 and later the abilities seoprostats/markers and seoprostats/annotate; demo data has notes too.
* New: search engine updates on the timeline, off until switched on under Settings → Data: Google's ranking updates with their type (core, spam, reviews and others) and its crawling, indexing and serving incidents, fetched from its Search Status Dashboard once a day by the daily job (never on visitors' pages; nothing about the site is sent), with past ones added the first time, and posts naming an update from other RSS, Atom or JSON feeds you add. A bar in the markers lane shows each rollout, including ones that began before the period; Changes has a Search engines group and links each update to its source. Kind search_update, group search; wp seoprostats search-updates fetch and status; wp seoprostats doctor shows failed sources, which are asked again the next day. Demo data has made-up updates too.
* New: Google Search Console, off until connected under the new Settings → Connections tab with a Google Cloud service account's JSON key, stored encrypted (libsodium; SEOPROSTATS_ENCRYPTION_KEY in wp-config.php, else the site's security keys) and never shown again. Clicks, impressions and average position by page, query, page and query, and device and country are imported for each final day (never preliminary figures), with the 16 months Search Console keeps imported on connecting, newest first, by WP-Cron within a 20-second budget per run; never on visitors' pages. Each import is listed and can be undone; Import now fetches what is ready. Search data by page and query is kept 25 months (Settings → Data); daily totals are kept. REST routes connections and imports; wp seoprostats connect, disconnect and search-console (status, import, imports, undo, reimport); wp seoprostats doctor shows its state; seoprostats_search_import_limits filter. The tables update by themselves.
